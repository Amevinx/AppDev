# My Favorite Movie: Hello, Love, Goodbye 💖

![Hello, Love, Goodbye](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/5a/Hello%2C_Love%2C_Goodbye_%28film%29_poster.jpg)

## 🎬 Overview
*Hello, Love, Goodbye* is a 2019 Filipino romantic drama film directed by *Cathy Garcia-Molina*, starring *Kathryn Bernardo* and *Alden Richards*.  
The movie tells the story of *Joy*, a hardworking OFW in Hong Kong, who crosses paths with *Ethan*, a bartender who dreams of settling down.

## 💡 Why I Like It
- It shows the sacrifices and struggles of OFWs.  
- The chemistry between the main characters feels natural and emotional.  
- It teaches lessons about love, dreams, and independence.  

## 💞 Main Characters
1. Joy Fabregas (Kathryn Bernardo)  
2. Ethan Del Rosario (Alden Richards)  
3. Mary Dale Fabregas (Maymay Entrata)  
4. Pinoy OFWs in Hong Kong  

*“Mahal kita, pero ako muna.”* – **Joy Fabregas**
